HOW TO CONTACT US: If you have questions, feedback, or need troubleshooting assistance, please reach out directly: • Email: brxtcopenhagen@gmail.com FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS: Q: How do I test the application? A: HDR Tricker offers a 2.5-minute free trial of the brightness multiplier. Slide the controls past 1.0 to witness the peak brightness capability of your HDR display. After 2.5 minutes, you will be prompted to purchase the Premium version to continue. Q: Why isn't my screen getting brighter? A: HDR Tricker works by mapping extended dynamic range (EDR) values across system-level gamma tables. To see the effect, you must be using an HDR-capable monitor, such as a MacBook Pro with a Liquid Retina XDR screen (M-Series, 2021 or newer), an Apple Pro Display XDR, or a compatible external HDR display. Q: Will this damage my screen? A: HDR Tricker utilizes standard public display APIs and operates safely alongside your Mac's built-in thermal safety limiters. However, running any display at maximum peak brightness for prolonged periods will increase battery drain and thermal output. Use responsibly.
